Starting LT listed questionable (knee)

By Sam Fixture, Sports Wire staff · Friday, November 6, 2026, 5:45 PM ET

The Buffalo Bills listed their starting left tackle as questionable for Sunday's game against the Kansas City Chiefs at Orchard Park. The team's final injury report gives a knee injury as the reason.

The tackle was limited in practice on Wednesday and Thursday and did not practice on Friday. The head coach said the decision will come on game day. "We will see how he feels Sunday morning," he said. "He has worked hard all week."

If he cannot play, the swing tackle is expected to start. The swing tackle has made two starts this season, both in October. The Bills have given up 14 sacks in nine games, one of the lowest totals in the league.

Kansas City brings one of the league's better pass rushes to Buffalo. The Chiefs have 27 sacks this season, and their edge rushers have combined for 15 of them.

Kickoff is set for 1:00 PM ET on Sunday, November 8, 2026. The Bills are 6-3 and the Chiefs are 7-2. The two teams have met in the playoffs in three of the last five seasons.

The Bills also listed a reserve linebacker as out with an ankle injury. The Chiefs have no players listed as out.
